# NP Catalogs: Brands

Category: Catalogs · Article 1040 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Brands Catalog in NeonPanel brings structure and depth to your inventory management and enables a range of performance analytics.

## Introduction

In NeonPanel, the Brands Catalog is a centralized repository that facilitates the management of different brands associated with your inventory. Through this catalog, you can efficiently add new brands and manage existing ones, thereby enriching your data for advanced analytics and reporting.

Upon activating the Amazon SP-API Integration in NeonPanel, the Brands Catalog is automatically populated with the brands that NeonPanel identifies in your Amazon Seller Central

account. Furthermore, the brand attribute is also automatically assigned to the items within the Products Catalog.

## Adding and Assigning Brands

If any brands are missed during the automatic population, you can manually add them to the Brands Catalog. Simply navigate to the Brands Catalog, click the "Add Brand" button, and fill in the required fields. The two fields to complete are: the brand name, which is mandatory, and the brand logo, which is optional.

After adding any missed brands, ensure that all products in the Products Catalog have assigned brands for consistent data representation.

## Brand Performance Analysis

Assigning brands to products is an essential organizational practice. This serves as a grouping mechanism and unlocks an array of analytical possibilities.

With the brand classifier in place, you can delve into various aspects of brand performance such as ad analytics, sales trends, inventory planning, refund analytics, etc. This data is invaluable for making informed decisions and strategically steering your brand's growth.
