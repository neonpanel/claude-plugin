# Merging Warehouses and Vendors

Category: Catalogs · Article 1131 · Updated 2026-02-25

This article explains how to merge warehouses and vendors in NeonPanel.

## Introduction

Merging warehouses is important during onboarding when you are setting up your warehouses and vendors catalogs, but can also be needed during business as usual (BAU), if you start using new warehouses to send inventory to FBA. This article explains how to merge warehouses.

Caution! Merging warehouses and vendors isn't something you can reverse, so be careful doing so.

## How to Merge Warehouses

In our hypothetical example, we have two warehouses - Supplier Forwarding and China Forwarding - that we want to merge.

In order to do so, you need to choose a warehouse you want to be deleted after the merger, and click on it. In this case, I want to keep Supplier Forwarding and delete China Forwarding, so I open the latter and click the 'Delete' button in the bottom left side of the screen.

Once I do, the system will warn me that there are transactions associated with this warehouse and it can't be deleted, and will ask me whether I want to first transfer transactions to another warehouse before deleting. I do, so I click 'Confirm'.

Next, I need to choose a warehouse to merge transactions to from the dropdown, so I choose Supplier Forwarding, and click 'Save'.

After that I get a confirmation message that transactions have been successfully transferred.

We are almost done. The only thing that is left is to once again click 'Delete' in the bottom right of the popup window and confirm you want to delete. Now that there are no transactions on this warehouse, they system will allow you to do so.

That's it! We have successfully merged the two warehouses together and we only have Supplier Forwarding left, which now has two addresses associated with it.

Next time an FBA Shipment with either of these addresses appears in the system, NeonPanel will know to associate it with this warehouse.

## Merging Vendors

Merging vendors in NeonPanel requires you to follow the exact same procedure as I have just shown with warehouses.
