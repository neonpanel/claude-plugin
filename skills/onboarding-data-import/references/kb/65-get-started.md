## Introduction

We think that process matters, especially when your business grows and becomes harder to manage. There are pre-built processes that are central to inventory management.

We collaborated with AmzNest (an Amazon incubator with vast experience with FBA Selling) to offer 3 important processes:

Inventory Order here you capture information about the new inventory you have ordered, including costs for shipping, inventory itself, brokerage, etc; these will be mostly used for COGS calculations.
