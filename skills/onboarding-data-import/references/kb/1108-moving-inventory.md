## Accuracy of Dates and Quantities

Effectively managing inventory movement is key to maintaining precise stock levels across various locations, which in turn enables NeonPanel to accurately automate landed costs and COGS calculations.

The consistency of dates in your Inventory Orders and Shipments is vital for maintaining an accurate FIFO (First-In, First-Out) chain. Inaccurate dates can disrupt this chain, leading to potential inventory management issues. For instance, creating a Shipment from a warehouse that is recorded as having no stock results in NeonPanel sourcing inventory from the 'Lost & Found' warehouse.

This approach comes with a significant drawback: the absence of landed costs. This happens because NeonPanel cannot trace the specific batch the inventory originated from, leaving a gap in cost data. Hence, ensuring date accuracy in your inventory management process is crucial for the integrity of your stock tracking and cost accounting in NeonPanel.

You can check the quality of your FIFO chain by looking at the Inventory Transactions report, and go to the COGS Quality tab.

## Conclusion

Mastering the process of moving inventory within NeonPanel is as crucial as managing new inventory orders.
