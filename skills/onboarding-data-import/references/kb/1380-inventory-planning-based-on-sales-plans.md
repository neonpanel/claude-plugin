# Inventory Planning Based on Sales Plans

Category: Inventory Management · Article 1380 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonPanel enables efficient inventory planning by integrating your sales plans, which you can upload via CSV or API after creating them using your team’s tools. The system calculates inventory balances and generates PO schedules based on your sales data, ensuring alignment with your planning horizon. Proper data formatting and adherence to template guidelines are essential for a smooth process.

## Introduction (Part 1)

Overview At NeonPanel, we believe that effective inventory planning begins with robust sales planning. A sales plan is more than a forecast; it requires in-depth analysis of market trends, competitive surroundings, product strategies, and resource allocation, including financial and promotional budgets. While NeonPanel doesn’t provide advanced tools for creating sales plans, we offer robust features for uploading and managing your sales plans. Our system integrates your uploaded sales plans with inventory balances, planning parameters, and PO schedules for seamless inventory management. Step-by-Step Guide to Uploading Sales Plans in NeonPanel 1. Preparing Your Sales Plan • Create your sales plan outside NeonPanel using your team and tools. • Ensure your plan considers: • Market trends and analytics. • Competitive positioning. • Product-specific strategies. • Financial and promotional budgets. NeonPanel provides historical sales data, profitability analytics, and ads performance insights to help you refine your planning, but the creation of the sales plan itself is your responsibility. 2. Download the Sales Plan Template • Go to Setup & Settings > Forecasts in NeonPanel. • Download the CSV template provided. 3. Completing the Template Open the downloaded file in your preferred spreadsheet software (e.g., Excel, Google Sheets). Follow these guidelines: • Company: Enter the exact company name as registered in NeonPanel. • Marketplace: Use accepted marketplace codes like US, UK, or EU. • Name: Provide a unique and descriptive name for the forecast (e.g., “Plan” for the main scenario). • Version: Include a version name or number (e.g., “actual” for the main required
