# Step 3. Optimize Inventory & Product Catalogs

Category: Initial Setup · Article 1200 · Updated 2026-02-25

This guide helps you identify and merge duplicate SKUs, ASINs, and FnSKUs, and handle non-standard SKUs for efficient inventory management.

## Introduction

When you first connect your Amazon account, NeonPanel will populate the Product and Inventory catalogs automatically using various reports provided by Amazon. We don't just recreate your Amazon catalog in NeonPanel; instead, we use reports such as Inventory Ledger, Amazon Statements and many more. In these reports, Amazon provides SKUs and FNSKUs of products the records are associated with, and when NeonPanel encouters a unique combination, we create a Product and an Inventory using them.

Amazon can be messy, and therefore, a lot of times the same SKU can come through with multiple FNSKUs, there can be multiple SKUs for a single FNSKU, or Amazon might even create their own SKUs.

Ensuring that your inventory and product catalogs are optimized is essential for efficient inventory management and maintaining accurate records. Below are common issues to look out for and practical steps you can take to address them using NeonPanel.

## 1. Multiple SKUs for One FnSKU on the Same Marketplace

What to Check: Are there multiple SKUs linked to a single FnSKU?

Why It Matters: Each FnSKU should uniquely represent a specific item configuration and packaging. Multiple SKUs for a single FnSKU usually indicate a data entry error or misconfiguration.

## How to Fix:

- Identify the SKUs linked to the same FnSKU.

- Merge the inventory items, designating the standard SKU as the parent.

- If one SKU is outdated, make the active SKU the parent and mark the outdated SKU as inactive.

- For detailed instructions on how to merge inventory items, refer to Merging Inventory Items.

- For assistance in finding such situations, please use NP's special report designed to help find duplicates.

## 2. Multiple ASINs for One SKU on the Same Marketplace

What to Check: Does one SKU have multiple ASINs associated with it?

Why It Matters: This can occur due to listing errors or similar products being listed under the same SKU, complicating inventory management.

## How to Fix:

- Identify the ASINs linked to the same SKU.

- Merge the inventory items by selecting one 'active' ASIN for the SKU and marking other ASINs as inactive.

- For detailed instructions on how to merge inventory items, refer to Merging Inventory Items.

## 3. Identification and Handling of Non-Standard SKUs

What to Check: Are there any non-standard SKUs (e.g., "amazozx.xxxx", "incompxxxx.xxx", "ASIN.Missing")?

Why It Matters: Non-standard SKUs can be generated by errors or automated system processes and must be addressed to maintain catalog integrity.

## How to Fix:

- Identify non-standard SKUs.

- Merge the product items by selecting a standard SKU as the parent for each non-standard SKU.

- For detailed instructions on how to merge product items, refer to Merging Product Items.

## 4. Multiple FnSKUs for One ASIN on the Same Marketplace

What to Check: Are there multiple FnSKUs associated with a single ASIN?

Why It Matters: Each scenario should be examined to ensure it aligns with operational intentions and Amazon’s policies, simplifying inventory management.

## How to Fix:

- Identify the FnSKUs linked to the same ASIN.

- Merge the inventory items by selecting one FnSKU to remain active and marking the others as inactive, ensuring compliance with Amazon policies.

- For detailed instructions on how to merge inventory items, refer to Merging Inventory Items.

By following these steps, you can maintain an organized and accurate inventory, ensuring that your product catalogs are optimized for efficient management.
