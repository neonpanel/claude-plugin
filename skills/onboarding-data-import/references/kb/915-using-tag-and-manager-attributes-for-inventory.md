# Using Tag and Manager Attributes for Inventory

Category: Tips & Tricks · Article 915 · Updated 2026-02-25

In NeonPanel, you can assign unlimited tags to inventory items for flexible analysis and a single manager for accountability. This helps in categorizing products, tracking performance, and improving inventory management.

## Introduction

Effective inventory management is essential for driving business insights and improving operational efficiency. NeonPanel offers a powerful feature that allows you to assign Tags and Managers to your inventory items, providing additional flexibility for analysis and management. This guide will walk you through the process of assigning Tags and Managers, and explain how these features can enhance your inventory tracking and reporting capabilities.

## Tags: Enhancing Flexibility in Analysis

Tags in NeonPanel are versatile labels that can be assigned to any inventory item. They allow you to categorize and segment your inventory in ways that are meaningful to your business.

Unlimited Tagging: You can assign an unlimited number of tags to any one inventory item, giving you the flexibility to categorize your products based on various criteria.

Use Case Examples:'Money Maker': A tag used for established, high-performing products.'Just Launched': A tag for newly launched products that are still ramping up in the market.

'Money Maker': A tag used for established, high-performing products.

'Just Launched': A tag for newly launched products that are still ramping up in the market.

These tags can later be used to filter and analyze your inventory, providing valuable insights. For example, you might want to exclude 'Just Launched' products from certain profitability analyses to focus on the performance of established products. Alternatively, you might use tags to show investors what your profits would look like if you cut new product development entirely.

## Managers: Streamlining Responsibility and Accountability

In addition to tags, NeonPanel allows you to assign a Manager to each inventory item. This feature is crucial for tracking responsibility and performance across your team.

Single Manager Assignment: Unlike tags, only one manager can be assigned to each inventory item. This ensures clear accountability for the management of each product.

Managerial Analysis: Assigning managers to inventory items allows for focused analysis. Managers can filter reports to view only their assigned products, and higher-level executives like CEOs or supervisors can filter inventory by specific managers to assess their effectiveness.

## Step 1: Access the Inventory Catalog

To begin assigning tags and managers, navigate to the Inventory Catalog in NeonPanel. This catalog is where you manage and organize all your inventory items.

## Step 2: Assign Tags and Managers Individually

Select an Inventory Item: Choose the inventory item you wish to assign tags or a manager to.

Assign Tags: Add one or more tags that categorize the product according to your business needs. There is no limit to the number of tags you can assign.

Assign a Manager: Select the manager responsible for this inventory item. Remember, only one manager can be assigned per item.

## Step 3: Bulk Edit and Assign

NeonPanel also offers a bulk edit feature, allowing you to assign tags and managers to multiple inventory items simultaneously.

Filter and Select: Use filters such as product families, categories, or other criteria to narrow down your inventory list.

Bulk Assign: Once you have selected multiple items, you can assign tags or a manager to all of them at once. This feature is particularly useful for large-scale inventory updates or when onboarding new products.

## Benefits of Using Tags and Managers in Inventory Analysis

Enhanced Reporting: Tags allow you to create more nuanced reports that reflect the specific categories and characteristics of your inventory. This can lead to deeper insights and more informed decision-making.

Improved Accountability: Assigning managers to inventory items streamlines responsibility, making it easier to track performance and manage workloads.
