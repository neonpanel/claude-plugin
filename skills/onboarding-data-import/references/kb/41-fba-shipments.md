# FBA Shipments

Category: Processes · Article 41 · Updated 2026-02-25

The FBA Shipment process is used to effectively manage your FBA shipments and to accurately record inventory movements to Amazon.

## Introduction

NeonPanel offers a streamlined process for managing FBA Shipments, making it easier for you to stay on top of your inventory.

FBA Shipments are automatically created inside NeonPanel once you create them in Seller Central, given that you have activated the Amazon Integration.

Once an FBA Shipment's status changes to 'Shipped' or 'In Transit', NeonPanel will create Inventory Management (IM) transactions based on the quantities entered when creating the shipment in Amazon Seller Central or other tools.

The quantities of each item will be deducted from the Origin Warehouse in the FBA Shipment Info task.

The quantities of each item will be added (moved to) the Amazon FBA warehouse - Destination Warehouse in the FBA Shipment Info task.
