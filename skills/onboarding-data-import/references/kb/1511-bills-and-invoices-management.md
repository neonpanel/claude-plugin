# Bills and Invoices Management

Category: accounting · Article 1511 · Updated 2026-07-21

## Bills vs Invoices

Bills vs Invoices:
- Bills: What you receive from vendors for goods/services you've purchased
- Invoices: What you send to customers for goods/services they've purchased

Key differences:
- Bills are incoming, invoices are outgoing
- Bills represent your expenses, invoices represent your revenue
- Bills are recorded as accounts payable, invoices as accounts receivable

## Missing Bill Troubleshooting

If a bill is missing:
1. Check vendor payment status in the accounting system
2. Verify the bill was properly entered with correct vendor information
3. Look for duplicate entries that might be hiding the original
4. Check if the bill was categorized under a different vendor name

## Missing Invoice Troubleshooting

If an invoice is missing:
1. Verify the customer was properly set up in the system
2. Check if the invoice was marked as paid or voided
3. Look for partial or split invoices that might be related
4. Confirm the invoice date falls within the expected billing period
