# Step 2: Finalise CoA and Service-Account Mapping

Category: QuickBooks/Xero Setup · Article 1448 · Updated 2026-02-25

Choose and finalize your preferred Chart of Accounts, import it to NP, and remap your service-account mapping.

## Introduction

Integrating your QBO or Xero account with NeonPanel and syncing data is straightforward. First, decide whether you want to use your existing Chart of Accounts (CoA), adopt NeonPanel’s default CoA, or use a combination of both.

Once you’ve made your decision, finalize your CoA on the QBO/Xero side and then import the accounts into NeonPanel. After importing, you must reconfigure the Service-Account mapping.

Note: Explanation on how to import your accounts is given in the article Step 3: Import Vendors, Accounts & Customers. In order to import the accounts, you first need to connect your accounting software to NP.

By default, NeonPanel maps its services to its default accounts; however, if you modify the CoA, you will need to remap the services to your selected accounts to ensure the correct accounts are used in your accounting transactions.

It is crucial to complete your CoA and service-account mapping before syncing data to QBO or Xero to ensure that the appropriate accounts are used and the correct transactions are created.
