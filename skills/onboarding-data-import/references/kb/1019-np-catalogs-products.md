# NP Catalogs: Products

Category: Catalogs · Article 1019 · Updated 2026-02-25

Products catalog is a central repository of SKUs for your account. It contains information such as brand, vendor, product family tag and much more.

## Introduction (Part 1)

NeonPanel's features are designed to bring structure, efficiency, and clarity to your Amazon selling experience. Today, we focus on one of the most essential elements of the platform - the Products catalog. Let's explore how it works, where it fits into your operations, and how to utilize it to its full potential. The Products catalog centralizes all your product data across multiple Amazon accounts, if applicable. Each product has a unique SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) for precise tracking. Regardless of the number of Amazon accounts you manage, this catalog provides an immediate overview of all SKUs. Note: In NeonPanel, products differ from inventory. Products are abstract entities or templates, whereas inventory items are specific products purchased for sale on a certain marketplace. When creating an inventory item, you select a product it's based on. The Products catalog offers more than just a list; it is a comprehensive tool reflecting your entire product portfolio. Each entry includes crucial product details such as description, price, availability status, associated vendors, etc. The Products catalog is particularly useful in the following ways: Inventory Management: The catalog forms an essential building block for NeonPanel's inventory management module, as it tracks all the SKUs related to your business.
