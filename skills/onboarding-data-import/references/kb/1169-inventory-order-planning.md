# Inventory Order Planning

Category: Reports · Article 1169 · Updated 2026-02-25

In NeonPanel user can automatically plan inventory order dates based on their sales plans.

## Introduction

NeonPanel offers a robust inventory planning algorithm designed to help businesses manage their inventory more effectively using advanced forecasting techniques and precise inventory calculations. This guide provides an overview of how to utilize NeonPanel's planning features to optimize your inventory management.

## Overview of Inventory Planning Features

NeonPanel's inventory planning capabilities are centered around a sophisticated algorithm that leverages sales forecasts and planning parameters to generate accurate inventory recommendations. By integrating these tools, businesses can streamline operations, reduce stockouts, and maintain optimal inventory levels.

## Getting Started with Inventory Planning

To effectively use NeonPanel's inventory planning algorithm, you need to complete the following steps:

## 1. Upload Your Sales Forecast

Sales Forecast: Begin by uploading your sales forecast into NeonPanel. This data serves as the foundation for the planning algorithm, enabling it to generate predictions based on projected demand.

## 2. Enter/Upload Planning Parameters

Safety Stock Days: Enter the number of safety stock days for each inventory item in the inventory catalog parameters. This value represents the buffer stock maintained to prevent stockouts due to demand fluctuations or supply chain disruptions.

## Automated Recalculation Schedule

NeonPanel ensures that your inventory decisions are based on the most current information by recalculating planning data twice daily. Here’s how it works:

Twice Daily Updates: The system updates inventory planning data twice a day, at midnight Los Angeles (LA) time and midnight UK time. This schedule guarantees that users across different time zones start their business day with the most up-to-date information.
