# Declaring intent instead of clicking menus

Category: Getting Started · Article 1538 · Updated 2026-06-20

How to get value from NeonaSphera: state what you want in plain language and let the assistant apply the right expertise.

## The core idea

NeonaSphera is designed so you **declare intent in conversation** rather than navigating menus. Instead of finding the right report, filter, and export, you ask: "What was my UK-market margin last month?" or "Which SKUs should I reorder this week?"

## How a conversation flows

1. **Start with what you want** — if you are unsure which area applies, say so and the assistant explains the platform or picks the matching expertise.
2. **Name the domain** when you know it — e.g. accounting — so the answer starts in the right place.
3. **Keep cross-domain questions in one conversation** — if your question spans domains (forecast → replenishment), the assistant carries the context across without you re-explaining.
4. **Ask the next question anytime** — a follow-up can be about a different area.

## Tips for better results

- Be specific about **time period, market, and product** when asking data questions.
- Say **what outcome you want** ("explain", "recommend", "update") not just the topic.
- If the assistant asks a clarifying question, answer it — answers improve with context.
- Use the **Knowledge Base** articles (or just ask) when you need platform how-tos.
