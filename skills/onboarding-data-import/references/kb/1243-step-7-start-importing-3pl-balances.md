# Step 7: Start Importing 3PL Balances

Category: Initial Setup · Article 1243 · Updated 2026-02-25

This article advises users to regularly import 3PL stock balances into NeonPanel, enabling comparison with system-calculated data to detect discrepancies and ensure accurate inventory management.

## Introduction

In this final step, we advise you on the ongoing maintenance of your inventory data to ensure its accuracy and reliability. In addition to the detailed Inventory Management Transactions data track that NeonPanel calculates, we also maintain a simplified 3PL actual data track. This dual-tracking system allows you to compare and contrast data for improved accuracy and error detection.

## Purpose

Regularly importing 3PL stock balances allows you to:

Compare NeonPanel-calculated data with third-party vendor reports.

Identify and resolve discrepancies between your calculated inventory and actual stock levels reported by 3PLs.

Maintain accurate and up-to-date inventory records.

## Process Overview

Amazon Inventory Data: NeonPanel automatically downloads daily inventory data from Amazon using their ledger data.

Non-Amazon Warehouse Data: Users can import 3PL stock balances for their non-Amazon warehouses.

We recommend uploading these balances at least twice a week for optimal accuracy.

## 1. Amazon Inventory Data

Automated Daily Updates: NeonPanel automatically updates Amazon inventory data daily, ensuring that your Amazon warehouse data is always current.

## 2. Non-Amazon Warehouse Data

Import 3PL Stock Balances: Users have the option to manually import stock balances for non-Amazon warehouses. Refer to the article Import 3PL Stock Balances for detailed instructions.

Frequency: We recommend uploading these balances at least twice a week.

## 3. Compare and Contrast Data Tracks

Inventory Audit Report:Utilize NeonPanel's Inventory Audit report to compare and contrast the data from both tracks. This report contains:Tabs with information specific to each data track.A combined tab where both tracks are considered simultaneously, allowing you to identify and resolve discrepancies.

## Benefits of Importing 3PL Stock Balances

Regularly importing 3PL stock balances provides several key benefits:

Error Detection: By comparing the data calculated by NeonPanel with the data reported by third-party vendors, you can identify potential errors on either side. For example, third-party providers may lose items, leading to lower reported values for some SKUs, which can be detected by discrepancies in the Inventory Audit report.

Data Accuracy: Ensures that your inventory management system reflects the true state of your stock, capturing discrepancies between NeonPanel-calculated data and third-party reports.

Operational Efficiency: Accurate inventory data helps in making informed decisions, improving overall operational efficiency.

Problem Identification: Helps in identifying problems such as lost inventory by third-party providers, as discrepancies between the actual and reported stock levels can highlight issues that need to be addressed.

## Conclusion

By completing this final step and regularly importing your 3PL stock balances, you can ensure that your inventory data in NeonPanel remains accurate and reliable. This practice allows you to compare and contrast NeonPanel-calculated data with third-party vendor reports using the Inventory Audit report, helping you identify and resolve any discrepancies. Regular maintenance of your inventory records is essential for effective inventory management and operational efficiency.
