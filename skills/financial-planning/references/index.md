## Knowledge areas

### Finance

File: `references/finance.md`

- **Answering a cash flow or spending question** — Procedure for forward-looking money questions: name the decision, verify the base is reconciled, state assumptions explicitly, and quantify the downside as well as the recommendation.
- **Where forward-looking finance ends and historical accounting begins** — Historical accounting produces and corrects the historical record; forward-looking finance reasons forward from it. Keep record-production work in historical accounting and never build a projection on unreconciled figures.
