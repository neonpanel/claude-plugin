# Landed Cost Analytics

Category: Reports · Article 1316 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Landed Cost Analytics Report tracks inventory costs, including purchase and logistics expenses. It features four tabs for data integrity, transaction details, shipment costs, and purchase order analysis, helping businesses optimize cost management and financial reporting.

## Introduction

This report is designed to help businesses effectively manage landed costs, which include the purchase price, shipping fees, duties, taxes, and any other costs incurred in getting goods to their final destination. Here's a closer look at each tab within the report:

## 1. Health Dashboard

The Health Dashboard offers an overview of the data quality related to landed costs. It provides:

Inconsistency Detection: Flags any discrepancies or gaps in landed cost data to ensure accuracy.

Data Integrity Checks: Highlights areas needing correction, enabling proactive management of financial records.

This tab is essential for maintaining the integrity of your cost data, ensuring that all financial reports reflect true inventory costs.

## 2. Cost Transactions List

The Cost Transactions List tab provides a detailed view of all transactions impacting landed costs. This includes:

Transaction Details: Lists each transaction affecting landed costs, along with the type, date, SKU, and related documents.

Transparency: Offers a comprehensive log that supports audits and financial reviews by detailing every cost-related transaction.

By using this tab, you can trace all cost-affecting transactions and ensure that landed costs are accurately recorded and managed.

## 3. Shipment Batch Details by SKU

The Shipment Batch Details by SKU tab offers a detailed breakdown of landed costs associated with each shipment. It includes:

Cost Breakdown: Shows how costs are divided between purchasing prices and logistics expenses for each shipment.

Batch Definition: Treats each shipment with an attached bill as a separate batch, assigning unique costs to each.

This tab allows businesses to understand how shipping and purchasing decisions affect overall inventory costs, providing insights for optimizing logistics and procurement strategies.

## 4. PO Batch Details by SKU

The PO Batch Details by SKU tab provides an alternative view of landed costs by focusing on purchase orders rather than individual shipments. This tab is designed to simplify analysis by treating costs as if they were directly linked to purchase orders. It includes:

Integrated Cost Analysis: Assesses landed costs at the purchase order level, simulating a scenario where bills are connected to POs rather than shipments.

Simplified Batch Tracking: Combines shipments into single batches based on purchase orders, distributing landed costs across entire inventory orders.

Weighted Average Allocation: If a shipment picks from multiple batches, costs are allocated proportionally based on the weighted average, simplifying the complexity of multiple entries.

This approach reduces the number of rows and complexity in the cost transactions list, making it easier to analyze the data and understand the financial impact of purchase orders as a whole. By simulating the integration of bills with POs, businesses can gain a clearer picture of cost distribution and manage inventory more effectively.
