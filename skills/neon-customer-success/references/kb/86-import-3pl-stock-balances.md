# Import 3PL Stock Balances

Category: Inventory Management · Article 86 · Updated 2026-02-25

In NeonPanel you can upload third-party warehouse inventory balances.

## Introduction

NeonPanel offers automatic daily updates for certain inventory balances, such as Amazon FBA inventories and those that are warehoused using the 3PL System (provided you enabled the integration).

If you are serviced by a warehouse that doesn't provide an API integration, you can manually upload the inventory as a CSV file.

You can import one or multiple files (.csv or .xlsx) with your inventory balances to be reflected in our Inventory Balances Report. Navigate to the Inventory Catalog tab, and click CSV Upload, then '3PL Warehouse Balances'.

## Requirements

To upload inventory balances, first download the template which is offered for you to download in the next window.

Fill out all the fields and click on the Import button. A few formatting tips:

Please fill out all the text fields such as Warehouse, Company exactly as they are written & spelled in NeonPanel.

Date field should have the following format: yyyy-mm-dd, e.g. 2024-12-31.

Marketplace column is to be filled out with entries such as: amazon.com (US), amazon.co.uk (UK), amazon.ca (Canada), amazon.de (Germany), etc.

## Uploading data for multiple companies

You can upload data for more than one company at a time by specifying more than one company name and the other corresponding information in the same file.
