# Talking to agents: declaring intent instead of clicking menus

Category: Getting Started · Article 1538 · Updated 2026-06-20

How to get value from NeonaSphera: state what you want in plain language and let the right agent execute.

## The core idea

NeonaSphera is designed so you **declare intent in conversation** rather than navigating menus. Instead of finding the right report, filter, and export, you ask: "What was my UK-market margin last month?" or "Which SKUs should I reorder this week?"

## How a conversation flows

1. **Start with Neona** if you are unsure — she explains the platform or routes you.
2. **Talk to a specialist** when you know the domain — e.g. open Marta for accounting.
3. **Let handoffs chain** — if your question spans domains (forecast → replenishment), agents can pass work to each other without you re-explaining.
4. **Return to Neona** anytime — specialists can hand back when your next question is outside their area.

## Tips for better results

- Be specific about **time period, market, and product** when asking data questions.
- Say **what outcome you want** ("explain", "recommend", "update") not just the topic.
- If the agent asks a clarifying question, answer it — routing improves with context.
- Use the **Knowledge Base** articles (or ask Neona) when you need platform how-tos.
