# Step 3: Import Vendors, Accounts & Customers

Category: QuickBooks/Xero Setup · Article 1456 · Updated 2026-02-25

This article explains how to import your Accounts, Customers and Vendors to NP.

## Introduction

After you have connected your Xero or QuickBooks account to NeonPanel, and finalized your chart of accounts (Steps 1 & 2), you need to import your Vendors, Accounts and Customers from your accounting software to NeonPanel.

This is easy to do. Navigate to your accounting software integration page, scroll down to the bottom, where the Import buttons are.

Click these buttons one by one, and your Vendors, Customers and Accounts will be imported into NeonPanel.
