# FIFO Transactions

Category: Reports · Article 1315 · Updated 2026-02-25

The FIFO Transactions Report tracks stock movements, sales, and purchase transactions. It highlights key insights on inventory control, COGS accuracy, and supply chain efficiency, helping businesses improve financial reporting and resolve discrepancies.

## Introduction

The FIFO Transactions Report is an essential tool for managing and analyzing stock movements within your business. This report provides detailed insights into the flow of inventory between warehouses, purchase orders, and invoice transactions, helping you maintain a clear picture of your stock levels and financial performance. By understanding this report, you can make informed decisions to optimize your inventory management and ensure the accuracy of your accounting records.

## Overview of the FIFO Transactions Report

The FIFO (First-In, First-Out) Transactions Report records all Inventory Management (IM) transactions within your system. It is divided into three main tabs, each providing unique insights into your inventory operations:

## 1. Transactions List

The Transactions List tab provides a comprehensive record of all stock movements and transactions that have occurred within your system. This includes:

Stock Movements: Detailed logs of inventory transfers between warehouses.

Purchase Orders: Transactions where stock is added to the system through procurement.

Invoice Transactions: Records of stock being sold or issued from the system.

This tab serves as a historical ledger, offering a complete view of your inventory activity. By analyzing the Transactions List, you can track the flow of goods, identify patterns, and spot discrepancies in stock levels.

## 2. COGS Quality

The COGS Quality tab focuses on sales-related transactions and their associated Cost of Goods Sold (COGS). It provides insights into:

Sales Transactions: Detailed data on each sale and the corresponding COGS for the selected period.

Lost COGS: For instances where the system cannot find corresponding stock for a sale, it estimates lost COGS based on the last received costs.

Supply Chain Management Quality: An evaluation of your supply chain's efficiency and effectiveness.

This tab is crucial for assessing the quality of your inventory management practices. It helps you understand how well you are managing your costs and highlights areas where improvements can be made. By examining the COGS Quality, you can identify inefficiencies, adjust pricing strategies, and enhance profitability.

## 3. Lost Batches

The Lost Batches tab highlights instances where transactions were recorded in the system, but no stock was available in the warehouse from which the goods were dispatched. Key aspects include:

Transaction Discrepancies: Identification of situations where recorded transactions do not align with actual stock levels.

Impact on COGS Quality: How lost batches contribute to lost COGS and affect overall accounting accuracy.

Shipment Issues: Analysis of shipments that resulted in lost batches, impacting inventory management and financial reporting.

Lost batches are a critical indicator of potential issues in your supply chain and inventory processes. While the current COGS Quality might seem satisfactory, a high number of lost batches can indicate future problems, leading to inaccuracies in accounting and financial statements. By addressing lost batches, you can improve the reliability of your inventory data and ensure a more accurate representation of your business's financial health.
