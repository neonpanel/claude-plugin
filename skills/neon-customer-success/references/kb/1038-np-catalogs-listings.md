# NP Catalogs: Listings

Category: Catalogs · Article 1038 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonPanel’s Listings Catalog is a centralised repository of your ASINs across various marketplaces within a specific company’s scope.

## Introduction

The Listings Catalog in NeonPanel offers a centralized overview of your Amazon listings in an organized table format.

It’s important to understand that the information displayed in the Listings Catalog is company-specific, meaning that it contains only the ASINs of the selected company.

Upon activating the Amazon Seller Central integration, the Listings Catalog automatically populates with an array of essential information regarding your ASINs, including: Product Name, ASIN, Color, Product Group, Product Type, Name, Publisher.

This structured approach not only makes the information easily accessible but also streamlines inventory management by maintaining Amazon’s familiar hierarchy. Additionally, this allows for filtering analytics and reports by ASIN or Parent ASIN, which is immensely helpful in focusing on specific products or groups of products for a more targeted and in-depth analysis.
