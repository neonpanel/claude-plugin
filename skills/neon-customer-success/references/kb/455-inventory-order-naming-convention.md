# Inventory Order Naming Convention

Category: Inventory Management · Article 455 · Updated 2026-02-25

Inventory orders should be named according to some convention for ease of reference. In this article, we provide an example

## Introduction

We recommend that you establish a naming convention for Inventory Orders as part of your process of Account Set Up. You are, of course, free to come up with your own. In case you need a simple, but effective example, please read on.

Imagine that you are creating an inventory order for a company Amazon Sellers United (ASU). The vendor is Asia Worldwide Manufacturing (AWM), and this is the twelfth (012) such shipment so far.

We recommend using the following structure:

Company Code-Shipment Number-Vendor CodeIn our example, we would name our inventory order: ASU-012-AWM.
