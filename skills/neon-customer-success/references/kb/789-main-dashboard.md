# Main Dashboard

Category: Dashboards · Article 789 · Updated 2026-02-25

Main dashboard is the place to see the valuation, net sales, margins, SDE - all the most critical financial metrics

## Introduction

Main Dashboard shows the most important financial metrics in NeonPanel. If you are aa CEO, CFO or an Investor, this dashboard will show you a summary of the financial position of the company.

## Filters & Settings

On the lefthand side of the tab, you can filter your analysis by company, marketplace, brand, product family and much more.

## Settings

On the righthand side are settings which will affect how your analytics are calculated.

First, you can choose whether to allocate Amazon Advertising expenses, storage fees and FBA inbound shipments to SKU level or not. If you choose to do so, we think you will get a more accurate representation of your product's true profitability.
