# Accounts & Companies

Category: NeonAdmin · Article 295 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonPanel provides consolidated business analytics, which is allowed by creating accounts

## Introduction

Only users with admin and/or superadmin role(s) have access to this functionality

Accounts are an architectural feature in NeonPanel, which was designed to allow for consolidated analytics. By creating an account, you can group all business data of your several companies for enterprise-wide analytics, providing you with a clear and comprehensive view of your business.

Read more about the different Analytics available to help understand your business performance.

## How to create an Account

To create a new account, go to NeonAdmin by clicking on your avatar in the top right corner and selecting Adminpanel.

Then, navigate to the Accounts tab and click Create Account.

All you need to complete this step is to provide a name for the account. You can add companies to it later from the Companies tab.

## How to create a Company

From the Companies tab within NeonAdmin, click on the Create Company button.

Next, you will see a page where you can add your Company Name, Logo, and other details, such as timezone, company address, default email for communication, and an account the company belongs to. Read more about what needs to be set up in NeonPanel once you have an account here.

## Why we created Accounts

Accounts were developed with the goal of facilitating reporting, analytics, and generating insights. By using this approach, our system can save users significant amounts of time by eliminating the need for manual data consolidation via CSV downloads and uploads.

Think of a company as your Amazon account. Larger sellers might have more than one account under management, and will likely want to view their inventory levels, profitability, or even business valuation in a consolidated manner. This is what the accounts are for.

If you have just one company, you still have an account - it was created when you set up your account. It will be easy to add it, should you need to!

## Accounts and accounting

NeonPanel has extensive functionality to automate accounting for our users. If you use QuickBooks, our QBO Integration will be useful for you.

It is worth mentioning that Accounts are 'virtual consolidators' and they don't have QBO accounts of their own. Instead, you connect each separate company to its respective QBO instance. The account will simply group all the data from different QBO accounts together.
