# Chart of Accounts

Category: Accounting · Article 1322 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Chart of Accounts page in NeonPanel offers a default CoA with Amazon-specific services for integrated accounts. Users can customize their CoA or adopt NeonPanel’s version for seamless integration with accounting software, ensuring accurate financial data and streamlined management.

## Introduction

The Chart of Accounts (CoA) page in NeonPanel is a vital tool for businesses aiming to achieve streamlined financial management and accurate accounting. This page allows users to manage and customize their accounting structures, ensuring that all financial transactions are recorded accurately and consistently across platforms. By offering a default CoA that includes Amazon-specific services, NeonPanel simplifies the process of integrating e-commerce transactions into your accounting workflows.

## Overview of the Chart of Accounts Page

The Chart of Accounts is a complete listing of every account in an accounting system. It organizes all accounts into categories, making it easier to record and report financial data. In NeonPanel, the CoA page provides a comprehensive view of all accounts, allowing users to make informed decisions about how financial transactions are managed and reported.
