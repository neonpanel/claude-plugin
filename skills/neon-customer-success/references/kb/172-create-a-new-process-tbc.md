## Introduction

Here is a little bit about how workflow works. NeonPanel workflow builder is based on 4 unique objects:

1. Processes - every business process can be set up as a separate NeonPanel Process which has a start time and no end time. Indefinite Process contains unlimited number of structured Projects.

Think of processes as templates.

2. Projects - are a logically separate, structured business task, which has a start date, an end date, has an owner, assignee, due date etc. The Project itself is comprised of Tasks.

Think of projects as typical business tasks

3. Task - a set of fields to add information to with detailed instructions. Tasks could be assigned to different users and can be set priorities and due dates.

Think of tasks as step-by-step to-do lists

4. Field - individual field in each task, meant to capture a discrete and important piece of business information

To structure complex indefinite business processes we divide them by projects. Every company has its own unique processes, but nevertheless, there are some pretty common ones.

To create your process you can import a template from the library, adjust it for you requirement and use it in your workflow.

You have the possibility to contribute their project templates to the NeonPanel community. If you think you built a good one, please contact us at. support@neonpanel.com, and we might make it available to all users.
