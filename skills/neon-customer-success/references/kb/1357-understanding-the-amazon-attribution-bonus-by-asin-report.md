# Understanding the Amazon Attribution Bonus by ASIN Report

Category: Reports · Article 1357 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Amazon Attribution Bonus by ASIN Report provides a detailed breakdown of both attributed sales and bonuses at the ASIN level. By combining data from Amazon’s Performance Report (which includes bonus information) and Product Report (which offers ASIN-level sales data), we automatically calculate and allocate the bonus per ASIN. This ready-to-use report saves users time and provides transparency into how external marketing efforts impact each product, enabling informed decisions for campaign optimization and product strategy.

## Introduction (Part 1)

The Amazon Attribution Bonus by ASIN Report is a comprehensive tool that provides a detailed breakdown of attributed sales and corresponding bonuses at the ASIN level. This report simplifies the process for users by combining data from Amazon’s Attribution reports and automatically calculating bonuses per ASIN, giving you a clear picture of the impact of your external marketing efforts. How We Prepare the Amazon Attribution Bonus by ASIN Report Our system pulls data from two key reports Amazon provides within their Attribution program: the Performance Report and the Product Report. Here’s how we utilize these reports to create the final, ready-to-use Amazon Attribution Bonus by ASIN report: 1. Performance Report (Bonus Information) The Performance Report offers insights into attributed sales, campaign metrics, and the bonus you receive for driving external traffic using Amazon Attribution tags. While this report includes bonus data, it does not break down the information by ASIN, which limits its usefulness for tracking individual product performance. 2. Product Report (ASIN-Level Data) The Product Report provides a detailed breakdown of attributed sales at the ASIN level. This allows us to see which products benefited from the external traffic, but it does not include the bonus information found in the Performance Report. 3.
