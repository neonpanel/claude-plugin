# Accounting Disclaimers

Category: Accounting · Article 761 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonPanel's accounting modules is a powerful solution to automate accounting and arrive at a single source of truth for the accounting data of your business.

## Introduction

Capture inventory procurement data in NeonPanel -> COGS & landed costs are automatically calculated & entered into QBO via journal entries (if you turn on the setting); landed costs are distributed to SKU level

Capture expenses data in NeonPanel -> landed cost automatically calculated & journal entries made; landed costs are distributed to SKU level & you control how (memo field in bills)
