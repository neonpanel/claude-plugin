# Step 1: Connect Amazon Accounts

Category: Initial Setup · Article 105 · Updated 2026-02-25

First thing to do in your accounts is to integrate your Seller Central and Amazon Ads accounts with NP.

## Introduction

You have just activated your NeonPanel accounts, congratulations. Here is the first step in leveraging its functionality - connect your Amazon Seller Central and Amazon Ads accounts.

Note: you need to add Seller Central and Amazon Ads accounts for each region where you actively sell goods.

## Amazon Seller Central

Please navigate the Amazon integrations page. On it, click Add Account.

You will be prompted to provide the following details:

Name - Will be used to display your company.

Region - Select the appropriate region depending on the marketplace you are selling on.

Start date - this is the date that will be used by NeonPanel to begin processing various data such as inventory transactions.

Once you have entered the required information, you will be directed to an Amazon authorization page. Here, you will need to sign in using your Amazon Seller Central account information.

After successful sign-in, you will be asked to authorize NeonPanel. To complete the integration setup, check the checkbox and click Confirm.

Upon completion, the integration will be successfully activated, and you can start leveraging its features.

## Amazon Ads

After activating the Amazon Seller Central integration, you may proceed with the addition of Amazon Ads integration. Navigate to the Integrations section of the main menu and select Amazon Seller Central. This will take you to the page containing your account information.

To connect the Amazon Ads, click on the Connect button.

Similar to the Amazon Seller Central integration process, you will be redirected to an Amazon Ads authorization page where you will need to sign in. Afterward, you will be prompted to authorize NeonPanel. To finalize the integration setup, check the checkbox and click Confirm.
