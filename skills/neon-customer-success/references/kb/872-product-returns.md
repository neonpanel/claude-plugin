# Product Returns

Category: Reports · Article 872 · Updated 2026-02-25

Product Returns is a report which gives you insight into the refund rates of your products at a specific time, or over time.

## Introduction

This report will help you better understand and analyze your refunds. In this reports you will see:

A  trend graph of refund rates for all items

Average refund rate for all products

A table with refunds rates for individual items (item types depend on grouping settings; in this case they are product families)

A visual that clearly shows the biggest contributors

Refund Rate = Total Refunds / Total Sales

## Trend Graph

Here you can see how your refunds percentages changed over time. This can help you to identify irregularities, persistently higher returns and other markers of product quality.

## Average Refund Rates

On the left hand side of this section you can see the average refunds rate for all items in your data. It can be a useful measure of product quality, or listing quality. If you average refunds rate went down, either your customers are more satisfied with your product, your listing better manages the expectations of your customers.

On the right hand side, you can see a breakdown of refund rates for all the items in your data for a specific period. They can help you identify which products specifically improved or got worse.

## The Biggest Contributors

This visual shows the product families with the highest sales for the period. This helps you to immediately identify impactful items. The higher the sales, the higher the absolute impact of a change in a refund rate.
