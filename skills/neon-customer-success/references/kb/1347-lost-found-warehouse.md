# Lost & Found Warehouse

Category: Inventory Management · Article 1347 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Lost & Found warehouse in NeonPanel records transactions with insufficient stock, flagging inventory discrepancies.

## Introduction

In inventory management, maintaining accurate records of stock levels is essential for smooth operations and financial accuracy. NeonPanel addresses discrepancies in stock availability with its Lost & Found warehouse feature.

This system warehouse is used to handle transactions where insufficient stock is recorded, ensuring that inventory inconsistencies are flagged and managed appropriately.

## What is the Lost & Found Warehouse?

The Lost & Found warehouse in NeonPanel is a virtual warehouse that serves as a placeholder for inventory transactions that cannot be fulfilled due to insufficient stock levels. It helps identify and manage discrepancies in stock data, ensuring that inventory records remain accurate and transparent.
