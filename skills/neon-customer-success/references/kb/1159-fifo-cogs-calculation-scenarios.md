## Description of FIFO Calculation Scenarios in NeonPanel

NeonPanel offers two distinct FIFO calculation scenarios designed to meet different business needs: the Standard version and the Basic version.

Standard Version: This scenario is ideal for businesses requiring detailed tracking of inventory costs across multiple warehouses. It supports comprehensive multi-warehouse inventory management, allowing businesses to maintain precise cost control and inventory tracking across various storage locations.

Basic Version: Designed for simplicity and ease of use, the Basic version incorporates significant simplifications to streamline inventory management. This version is particularly suited for businesses that prefer a straightforward approach and can forgo detailed tracking for each warehouse location. The key simplifications in the Basic version are:

Unified Warehouse Treatment: All inventory, regardless of its physical location, is treated as if it is located in a single warehouse. This approach simplifies the FIFO calculations and inventory management by eliminating the complexities associated with multiple warehouses.

Exclusion of FBA Inbound Shipment Transactions: The Basic version does not consider FBA Inbound Shipment transactions in the FIFO calculations. This omission further simplifies the accounting and inventory tracking process, making it easier to manage for users who do not require granular detail on every transaction.
