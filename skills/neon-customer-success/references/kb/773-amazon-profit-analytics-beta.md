There are seven dedicated tabs for specific analysis and a Filters & Settings tab that allows you to customize the analytics to your needs. Let's go through all the dashboard tabs.

## Filters & Settings

The Filters & Settings tab is the starting point for all analyses.

## Filters

The Filters are located on the left side of the tab and are used to narrow down the data you wish to analyze. For example, you may want to focus on a specific marketplace, brand, product family, etc.

## Settings

On the right side of the Filters & Settings tab are the Settings, which allow you to adjust the calculations for profitability. You can allocate Amazon Advertising expenses, storage fees, and FBA inbound shipments to the SKU level, providing a more accurate representation of your product's true profitability.

## KPI Dashboard

The KPI Dashboard provides a high-level overview of your company's profitability, displaying essential metrics such as

Net Sales

Seller Discretionary Earnings (SDE)

Margin

TACoS

## Details

The Details tab presents an extensive table of all your expenses, revenues, taxes, and other metrics that you can group by products, companies, marketplaces, and timeframes. This tab provides a deeper level of analysis, enabling you to drill down into the specifics of your business operations.

## Profit & Loss Dashboard

This chart displays the trend in profitability and costs of your business over time. You can easily filter by a specific product or set of products to analyze each product's profitability.

## SDE LTM Dashboard (coming soon)

SDE LTM stands for Seller Discretionary Earnings in the Last Twelve Months. This metric indicates how much excess cash your business has generated in a chosen period.

## COGS & Income Variations (coming soon)

The COGS Variations tab shows your total COGS for a selected period in different variations.

As COGS variance necessarily affects the income of your business, the Income Variations tab shows how your income would differ, depending on the method of calculating COGS.

The different methods of COGS calculation are covered in a separate article.
