# Transaction Map

Category: Accounting · Article 1353 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Transaction Map in NeonPanel allows users to map transaction events to specific accounts, ensuring accurate and customizable accounting entries. This feature allows for customizable mapping parameters, enhancing financial tracking tailored to business needs.

## Introduction (Part 1)

The Transaction Map functionality in NeonPanel offers a robust and flexible framework for users to map specific transaction events to their respective accounts within their chart of accounts. This capability ensures precise and customizable accounting entries, allowing businesses to maintain accurate financial records that reflect their unique operational workflows. Key Features Event MappingUsers can define and map various transaction events associated with different documents (e.g., bills, invoices, journal entries) to specific accounts within their chart of accounts.This feature provides a high level of customization, enabling users to align the financial recording process with their specific business needs. Event Mapping Users can define and map various transaction events associated with different documents (e.g., bills, invoices, journal entries) to specific accounts within their chart of accounts. This feature provides a high level of customization, enabling users to align the financial recording process with their specific business needs. Document-Specific MappingDifferent documents can trigger distinct transaction events that are mapped to appropriate accounts.For example, a bill may map to an expense account, while an invoice could trigger entries in both revenue and accounts receivable.
