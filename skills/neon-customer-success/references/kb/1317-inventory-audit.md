# Inventory Audit

Category: Reports · Article 1317 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Inventory Audit Report tracks inventory balances and values across warehouses. It features three tabs: Calculated by NeonPanel (internal data), Actual 3PL (third-party data integration), and Audit NeonPanel vs 3PL (compares internal and external data to identify discrepancies).

## Introduction

The Inventory Audit Report is an essential tool for businesses seeking to maintain accurate visibility of their inventory balances and values. This report allows users to drill down into inventory details at the level of individual warehouses on any chosen date, providing critical insights into stock management and control. With this report, businesses can effectively track inventory over time, ensure data accuracy, and maintain accountability with third-party logistics providers.

## Overview of the Inventory Audit Report

The Inventory Audit Report is designed to give users a comprehensive view of their inventory across multiple warehouses, integrating data from various sources to provide a clear and accurate picture of stock levels and value. The report consists of three main tabs:

## 1. Calculated by NeonPanel

The Calculated by NeonPanel tab presents all inventory balances as calculated by the NeonPanel system. This tab is based on data derived from your internal inventory management activities, including:

Inventory Orders: Reflects all incoming stock orders processed through NeonPanel.

Shipments: Includes all outgoing and transferred stock movements.

Invoices: Records the sale of inventory to ensure sales are accurately reflected in your inventory balances.

Internal Data Analysis: Utilizes data from your inventory management module to provide precise calculations of inventory balances.

This tab serves as the primary source of inventory data within your organization, offering an accurate representation of stock levels based on system records.

## 2. Actual 3PL

The Actual 3PL tab displays inventory data sourced from third-party logistics (3PL) providers. Key features include:

Automated Amazon Integration: NeonPanel automates the synchronization of inventory data with Amazon, providing real-time updates and ensuring that your inventory records are always current and accurate.

3PL Integrations: Connects with systems from third-party warehouses and logistics partners.

Uploaded Data: Allows for manual uploads of ledger data provided by your 3PL partners on a regular basis, with a recommendation for weekly updates.

This tab is crucial for incorporating external data into your inventory tracking, enabling businesses to reconcile internal records with information from logistics partners efficiently.

## 3. Audit NeonPanel vs 3PL

The Audit NeonPanel vs 3PL tab offers a comparative analysis of the two data streams, allowing you to:

Superimpose Data Streams: Overlay internal NeonPanel calculations with external 3PL data for direct comparison.

Identify Discrepancies: Quickly identify any differences between your internal records and those reported by 3PL providers.

Investigate Issues: Use discrepancies to trigger investigations, such as when a 3PL provider loses stock, allowing you to hold partners accountable and potentially save thousands of dollars.

This tab is invaluable for maintaining control and accountability over your inventory, ensuring that discrepancies are addressed promptly and that external data matches your internal records.

## Benefits of the Inventory Audit Report

Using the Inventory Audit Report provides several key benefits:

Enhanced Inventory Visibility: Gain a detailed view of stock levels across multiple warehouses, improving inventory management.

Automated Data Synchronization: Benefit from NeonPanel's automated integration with Amazon, ensuring that your inventory data is always up-to-date and accurate.

Data Accuracy and Control: Maintain accurate records by reconciling internal and external data sources.

Accountability with 3PL Providers: Ensure that third-party logistics partners accurately report inventory levels, protecting your business from losses.

Historical Tracking: Track inventory changes over time to better understand stock trends and performance.
