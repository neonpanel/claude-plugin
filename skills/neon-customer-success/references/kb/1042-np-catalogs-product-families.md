# NP Catalogs: Product Families

Category: Catalogs · Article 1042 · Updated 2026-02-25

Product Families Catalog in NeonPanel empowers users with an efficient approach to product classification, which is indispensable for effective analytical data aggregation across various marketplaces.

## Introduction

In NeonPanel, the Product Families Catalog serves as an essential tool for organizing and managing products effectively, especially when it comes to analytical data aggregation.

## What are Product Families?

Product families are tags created by users to conveniently group their products according to custom logic. Managers often want to analyze the performance of groups of SKUs, and the product families tag allows them to do just that. For example, if you have the same product sold on different marketplaces with different SKUs, but you still want to analyze them together, assigning both to a product family would allow you to do so.

## Creating and Managing Product Families

Access the Product Families Catalog from the navigation menu and click Add Family. Here, you can enter the name of the product family and optionally upload an image before saving.

Once created, you can assign products to these families through the Products Catalog. Simply select a product, and choose the appropriate product family from the dropdown menu.

Note that a product can only belong to one product family.

You can also create a new product family directly from the Products Catalog by clicking the "Add New Product Family" button.

## Why product families?

The primary reason for creating product families is to aggregate analytical data efficiently. Product families act as a mechanism for grouping different but related products, streamlining analysis.

For example, if you sell various bedroom accessories, you can create product families like 'Sleeping products' and 'Decorative products', or 'Blankets' and 'Pillows', depending on how you want to analyze data or perceive your business.

When you need to analyze metrics such as profitability or inventory levels, you can simply select the relevant product family. This aggregates all the products assigned to that family, saving you time and making it easier to generate insights about your business.
