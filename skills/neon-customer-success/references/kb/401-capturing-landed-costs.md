# Capturing Landed Costs

Category: Landed Cost Management · Article 401 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonPanel automates landed cost accounting via three processes: Inventory Orders, FBA Shipments, and Landed Cost Bills

## Introduction

A key advantage of NeonPanel is its ability to automate landed cost accounting, simplifying the process of tracking costs related to manufacturing and transportation of inventory to the point of sale.

NeonPanel utilizes the FIFO accounting method for determining COGS based on landed costs.

Unlike other industry software that requires manual calculations and importing of landed costs for various periods, NeonPanel automates these processes to provide users with valuable insights into ROI, margins, and profitability.

## Here is how it works

To enable automated landed cost calculations, users need to maintain and update up to three key processes (the 4th is fully automated):

1) Inventory Order2) Inventory Shipments & FBA Shipments3) Landed Cost Bills

For detailed information on creating and filling out these processes, please follow the corresponding links above.

Below, we examine how these processes, once complete, impact landed costs.

## Inventory Order

The Inventory Order process records the direct costs of goods, which typically constitute the largest portion of any inventory's landed costs.

## Inventory & FBA Shipment

The FBA Shipment & Inventory Shipment processes typically don't capture any landed costs directly, because they are designed primarily to move inventory rather than capture costs.

However, you can connect Bills to both FBA & Inventory shipments, which will result in NeonPanel capitalizing these costs, and distributing the overall amount down to individual item levels.

NeonPanel automates FBA Shipments for you. Just create an FBA Inbound Shipment in Seller Central - we'll do the rest.

## Landed Cost Bill

Landed Cost Bills account for freight costs as well as inspection, brokerage, customs duty and other associated costs that contribute to landed costs.

Expenses recorded in Landed Cost Bills are distributed to individual inventory items using the allocation method selected for a service used in the bill.

The 3 processes provide NeonPanel with sufficient data to automatically calculate COGS as soon as the sales information is imported to NP either via an integration or manually, provided you manage your inventory well.
