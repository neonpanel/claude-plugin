# Sync Documents to Xero Manually

Category: Xero Integration · Article 1461 · Updated 2026-02-25

This article explains how to synchronize documents generated in NeonPanel to your accounting software.

## Introduction

With a manual sync setting selected, users need to synchronize documents from the Register into their accounting software themselves. There are two options for doing so:

One document at a time

Multiple documents at once using batch action

To perform either, users first need to navigate to Register page in NeonPanel, which acts as a staging area for financial documents before those are syncing to the accounting software.

## Syncing one document at a time

To sync a document, click on the cogwheel next to it on the left, and in the dropdown select Sync.

## Syncing multiple documents at a time

In order to sync multiple documents at once, please first select all the documents you want to sync, then press the Batch Action button and then in the dropdown select Sync.
