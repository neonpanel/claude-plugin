Category: Processes · Article 54 · Updated 2026-02-25

Inventory order process is the foundation of NP, used to capture information about new inventory purchases and their costs.

## Introduction

NeonPanel provides a robust framework for managing inventory and accurately calculating costs through its Inventory Order process. This process is crucial for capturing detailed information about purchase orders and ensuring that every stage of inventory management is meticulously documented. Below, we break down the core stages and key tasks involved in the Inventory Order process.

## General Overview of the Inventory Order Process (Part 1)

The Inventory Order process in NeonPanel is divided into three main stages: Order Placement: This initial stage involves creating and submitting purchase orders. During this stage, important details about the order, such as vendor information, order dates, and expected delivery timelines, are recorded.
