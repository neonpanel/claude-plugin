## Introduction

Effective inventory management in NeonPanel is crucial for maintaining accurate financial records, especially when it comes to FIFO (First-In, First-Out) chain calculations.

This article provides an in-depth look at the Inventory Shipment process in NeonPanel, guiding you through each step to ensure precision in your inventory tracking and cost calculations.
