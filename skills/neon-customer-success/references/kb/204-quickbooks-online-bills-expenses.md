# QuickBooks Online Bills & Expenses

Category: QuickBooks Integration · Article 204 · Updated 2026-02-25

In NeonPanel you can track expenses and automatically synchronise them with QuickBooks Online

## Introduction

We use a process called Bills & Expenses to track expenses in NeonPanel. If you have QuickBooks Online Integration activated, all related documents are exported into QuickBooks Online.

This functionality is only available for Essentials QuickBooks Online subscription and above. To learn more, read out QuickBooks Online Settings article.

## Here is how it works

1) In NeonPanel we have a Services catalog.

It allows you to add services and set up service accounts to be used in QuickBooks Online. This catalog has two-way synchronization with QuickBooks Online, meaning that any service you create in NeonPanel will be automatically added to your QuickBooks Online account.

2) We use a system process called Bills & Expenses where you can enter your expenses that don't come from Amazon.

3) When you enter data in the Bills & Expenses project and tick the Service Items checkbox, this data is synchronized with QuickBooks Online, provided that you have the automatic setting enabled in the QuickBooks Online integration settings page.
