# NP Catalogs: Warehouses

Category: Catalogs · Article 1034 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Warehouses Catalog in NeonPanel is an invaluable tool that enables streamlined and centralized inventory management across various warehouses.

## Introduction

Managing inventory effectively is crucial for businesses to track movements, shipments, calculate landed costs, and maintain accurate inventory levels. NeonPanel's Warehouses Catalog serves as a fundamental stepping stone towards achieving this goal, providing a centralized space for warehouse management.

## Warehouse Information and Management

The Warehouses Catalog contains a list of warehouses with general information about them such as name, marketplace, vendor, and address. By adding warehouses to the catalog, you enable NeonPanel's inventory management system to track movements, shipments, calculate landed costs, and accurately reflect inventory levels associated with those warehouses. This, in turn, improves your analytics and ensures that your inventory management and analytics work to their full potential.

## Amazon FBA Warehouses

For businesses utilizing Amazon's FBA program, NeonPanel offers seamless integration via Amazon SP-API. Once you activate the integration, NeonPanel will automatically populate the Warehouses Catalog and sync the inventory balances with your Amazon warehouses. This automated process ensures that your stock levels are up to date. To verify the accuracy of the information, you can review the Inventory Balances report.

In case the automatic synchronization encounters any issues, you have the option to manually update your inventory balances in NeonPanel. You can find detailed instructions in our Import Inventory Balances article.

## Adding Non-Amazon Warehouses

If you have warehouses that are not affiliated with Amazon, you can manually add them to the Warehouses Catalog.

To add a non-Amazon warehouse, navigate to the catalog and click on the "Add Warehouse" button. Fill out the necessary profile details. While the name and marketplace are required fields, you can provide additional optional information.

After adding all your warehouses, we recommend to import inventory balances to ensure accurate tracking. Instructions for importing inventory balances can be found in this article.

If you have 3PL-based warehouses, you can contact us at support@neonpanel.com, and our team will assist you with integrating them with NeonPanel, automating and simplifying the process.
