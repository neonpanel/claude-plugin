# Step 4: Select Accounting Integration Settings

Category: QuickBooks/Xero Setup · Article 1458 · Updated 2026-02-25

This article outlines what settings you need to configure before you can sync your NP documents to your accounting software.

## Introduction

Next, you need to select and save the integration settings for your accounting integration. You can do that on your integration page.

In broad strokes, the are different sections for different types of projects in NeonPanel, and you can choose between Off (syncing not allowed), Manual (requires manual triggering to sync) and Auto (automatically syncs all documents from the register) settings.

You should also choose the 'Start Sync Date' to be in line with your Start Date that you had chosen when you connected your Amazon account.
