# How to Add a New Product in NeonPanel

Category: Catalog Management · Article 1512 · Updated 2026-02-25

Step-by-step guide for adding a new product to the NeonPanel catalog, including SKU setup, family assignment, and pricing configuration.

## Overview

This guide walks you through adding a new product to the NeonPanel catalog. Products are master records that define what you sell — they are separate from Inventory, which tracks stock levels by warehouse.

## Prerequisites

- Access to the Products section in NeonPanel
- Product name and SKU ready
- (Optional) A Product Family to assign the product to

## Steps

1. Navigate to the **Products** section in NeonPanel.
2. Click **"Add Product"** or **"New Product"**.
3. Enter the product name, SKU, and basic details.
4. Assign the product to a **Product Family** if applicable (optional).
5. Configure pricing and availability settings.
6. **Save** the product.
7. Verify the product appears in the catalog.

## What to Check After

- Confirm the product appears in the Products list with the correct SKU.
- If assigned to a Product Family, verify the family hierarchy shows the product.
- Note: Adding a product does **not** automatically create Inventory. You must set up Inventory separately to track stock levels.
