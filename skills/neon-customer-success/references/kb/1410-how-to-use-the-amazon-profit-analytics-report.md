# How to Use the Amazon Profit Analytics Report

Category: Reports · Article 1410 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Amazon Profit Analytics report offers two distinct modes to analyze your financial data effectively: Statement and Normal. Learn how to use it.

## Section

The Amazon Profit Analytics report offers two distinct modes to analyze your financial data effectively:

## 1. Statement Mode

Data Source: Uses data directly from Amazon Statements, presented as-is.

Cost Allocation: In this mode, Amazon Ads costs appear as they are reported in Amazon Statements, combined with inventory costs.

Ads Representation: Amazon Statements often show ad costs as discrete amounts (e.g., ~$500) without detailed breakdowns by ASIN/SKU. These invoices may also be recorded a few days before or after the ads were actually consumed.

When to Use: This mode is ideal when you need a financial overview that directly matches your Amazon Statement records, especially for accounting or financial reconciliation purposes.

## 2. Normal Mode

Data Source: Combines Amazon Statement data with Amazon Ads reports.

Cost Allocation: In this mode, NeonPanel intelligently distributes Amazon Ads costs based on consumption data from Amazon Ads reports.

Detailed Breakdown: Ads costs are shown by ASIN/SKU and aligned with the actual dates the ads were consumed, providing a more accurate reflection of marketing expenses.

When to Use: This mode is ideal for operational analysis, SKU-level profitability insights, and understanding the real impact of ads on individual products.

## Key Difference:

Statement Mode = Quick financial overview, aligned with Amazon Statement records.

Normal Mode = Detailed operational view with precise cost distribution by SKU/ASIN, ideal for brand managers and strategic analysis.

By switching between these modes in NeonPanel, you can tailor the report to your specific needs—whether you’re focused on financial reporting or detailed SKU-level performance insights.
