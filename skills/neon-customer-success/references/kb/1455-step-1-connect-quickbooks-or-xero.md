# Step 1: Connect QuickBooks or Xero

Category: QuickBooks/Xero Setup · Article 1455 · Updated 2026-02-25

This articles explains how to connect your accounting software to NeonPanel.

## Introduction

In order to connect your QuickBooks or Xero accounts to NeonPanel, please navigate to the QuickBooks or Xero integration pages. Once there, click on Connect to QuickBooks button (even if you have Xero).

Note: you should be logged in to the correct account in QuickBooks or Xero prior to integrating them with NeonPanel.

You will be redirected to the appropriate QuickBooks or Xero login page, if you are not logged in.

Next, you will be asked to confirm that you want to connect your software to NeonPanel. Click 'Connect' as in the screenshot below, or the Xero alternative.

Once done, you will be redicted to the integration page. Your accounting software is now connected.
