# Sold Inventory Batch Tracker

Category: Reports · Article 1318 · Updated 2026-02-25

This report provides detailed insights into the landed costs and sales timelines of inventory batches, helping teams make informed decisions about pricing and inventory management.

## Introduction

The Sold Inventory Batch Tracker report is a valuable tool for gaining insight into the landed costs and sales timelines of inventory batches. By focusing on a single inventory item, this report provides a detailed view of the costs associated with each batch, as well as the start and end dates of batch sales. This information is crucial for operations and marketing teams when making strategic decisions about product pricing, promotions, and inventory management.

## 1. Detailed Cost Tracking

The report allows you to track the landed costs of each inventory batch. Landed cost includes all expenses incurred to bring a product to your warehouse, such as shipping fees, duties, and taxes. Understanding these costs is essential for:

Pricing Decisions: Determine product pricing strategies based on accurate cost data.

Profitability Analysis: Assess the profitability of each batch by comparing costs to revenue generated.

## 2. Sales Timeline

The report provides visibility into when each batch started and finished selling. This timeline is important for:

Inventory Planning: Identify patterns in sales cycles to optimize inventory restocking.

Marketing Strategies: Plan promotions and marketing campaigns based on sales timelines and historical data.

## 3. Filter by Inventory

For optimal use, filter the report by a single inventory item. This focus provides a clear picture of:

Batch Performance: Evaluate the performance of each batch individually, allowing for precise analysis.

Operational Insights: Gain insights into how specific products are moving through the sales pipeline.

## 4. Historical Data Visibility

The report offers access to historical data, enabling teams to:

Trend Analysis: Analyze past sales performance and cost trends to forecast future demand.

Strategic Planning: Use historical insights to inform long-term strategic decisions, such as product launches and discontinuations.

## Benefits of the Sold Inventory Batch Tracker

Enhanced Decision-Making: Empower operations and marketing teams with precise cost and sales data to make informed decisions.

Cost Control: Monitor and manage landed costs effectively to maintain profitability.

Improved Inventory Management: Optimize inventory levels by understanding sales cycles and trends.

Strategic Insights: Leverage historical data to support strategic planning and competitive positioning.
