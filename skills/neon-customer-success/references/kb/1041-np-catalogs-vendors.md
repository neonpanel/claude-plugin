# NP Catalogs: Vendors

Category: Catalogs · Article 1041 · Updated 2026-02-25

The Vendors Catalog in NeonPanel is not only a centralized repository for vendor information but also serves as a foundation for advanced filtering and analysis through the use of Vendor Tags

## Introduction

In NeonPanel, the Vendors Catalog serves as a centralized hub designed to maintain and manage all your vendor information in a structured and easily accessible manner.

## Adding Vendors to the Catalog

To kickstart your experience with the Vendors Catalog, you’ll need to add your vendors’ profiles. This can be done by navigating to the Vendors Catalog within NeonPanel and clicking on the “Add Vendor” button.

You’ll find three tabs for entering vendor information. While only the main tab is mandatory, the other two are there for your convenience to store any additional vendor-related details.

## Utilizing Vendor Tags for Analysis

The Vendors Catalog is integrated with the Products Catalog through the use of "Vendor” tag, which acts as a mechanism for grouping products based on their respective vendors. This feature not only keeps things organized but also proves to be highly beneficial for different reports and analysis.

Refunds Analysis: You can analyze refunds grouped by vendor, allowing you to pinpoint if there are any trends or patterns associated with a specific vendor's products that might require attention.

Performance Analysis: The ability to evaluate the performance of products sourced from a particular vendor is essential. By employing vendor tags, you can observe how products from a specific vendor are performing, aiding in making informed decisions regarding inventory management and supplier relationships.
