# Intro to NP Analytics

Category: Analytics · Article 15 · Updated 2026-02-25

Numerous interactive dashboards will help you quickly analyze all dimensions of your business. You can also use reports for deeper analytical dives into specific issues

## Introduction

In NeonPanel there are two components of our Business Analytics module:

Dashboards

Reports

## Dashboards

Dashboards should be used as a main vehicle for analysing business performance. There are many dashboards in NeonPanel, and almost all have multiple tabs.

There are dashboards which help you analyze profitability, sales, inventory levels, financials, those that assist in planning inventory levels.

To get to know all our reports, visit the Dashboards KB page.

## Reports

Reports, on the other hand, help you conveniently view and analyze specific issues. For example, our Product Returns report helps you analyze which products have been returned the most, return percentages for specific products, and trends.
