# NeonAdmin Intro

Category: NeonAdmin · Article 702 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonAdmin is the place where you can manage your team members' roles & permissions, companies, accounts, and more

## Introduction

NeonAdmin is the user interface that our clients utilize to manage their teams. You may have already used it during the initial setup of your users & companies. To access NeonAdmin from NeonPanel click on your avatar in the top right corner and choose NeonAdmin.

With NeonAdmin, you have the ability to add, delete, or edit various aspects, such as:

users - used to invite new users, assign roles to them (which inherit permissions), and assign users to companies

roles & permissions - used to create different roles, such as accountant, product manager, data analyst, etc., and assign specific permissions to each of these roles

accounts & companies - used to establish relationships between your Amazon Accounts (if you have multiple)

## How Access Control Works

In addition to providing essential business analytics, NeonPanel also incorporates processes for capturing sensitive business data.  Our role-based access control (RBAC) system is designed to give maximum control and flexibility.

## Permissions

By default, all pages, tabs, reports, and other content within NeonPanel are locked. Permissions are required to gain access to these various components, with two types of access available: ReadOnly and Edit. ReadOnly access allows you to view inventory orders (or specific tasks within them), whereas Edit grants you permission to change any field in the project.

## Roles

Roles are configured with specific permissions that are required for each individual worker to fulfill their job duties. According to the principle of least privilege, it is best to give each worker only the amount of access necessary - neither more nor less. For example, a data analyst may only need permission to view the analytics section of the app or even just a specific report.

Roles determine access, not users. Users can be assigned roles, and therefore inherit the access a role grants.

## Users

Once you have set up the first two aspects of your preferred RBAC, you can assign roles to users.

One user can be assigned multiple roles.

User profiles also have companies attributes, which provides an additional lever of control in access to sensitive data. If you have multiple companies, some users may require access to data in all companies (such as a CEO), while others may only need access to data for one specific company (such as product managers). NeonPanel can easily accommodate both scenarios.
