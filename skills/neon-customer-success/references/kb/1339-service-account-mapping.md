# Service-Account Mapping

Category: Accounting · Article 1339 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonPanel's Service-Account Mapping aligns services with your Chart of Accounts (CoA) for accurate financial reporting. It includes Landed Cost services to capitalize expenses towards inventory costs, ensuring precise COGS and inventory valuation while matching Amazon’s Accounts Summaries.

## Introduction

Integrating financial data from complex platforms like Amazon is crucial for accurate accounting and financial management. NeonPanel facilitates this process through its Service-Account Mapping, which connects services to specific accounts in your Chart of Accounts (CoA). This feature ensures that your financial transactions are recorded correctly and consistently, providing reliable financial insights.

## Overview of Service-Account Mapping

In NeonPanel, Service-Account Mapping allows you to align the Services you use with your Chart of Accounts (CoA). This ensures financial transactions are accurately represented, enabling seamless auditing and reporting. Users can opt to retain their current CoA or adopt NeonPanel's default configuration.
