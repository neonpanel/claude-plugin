# Bills

Category: Processes · Article 545 · Updated 2026-02-25

NeonPanel's Bills process allows you to capture expenses and ensures accurate accounting

## Introduction

Bills & Expenses is an essential process in NeonPanel's accounting system, which captures expenses unrelated to the cost of goods sold (COGS).

In addition, NeonPanel offers a special type of bill called landed cost bill, which enables the capture of landed costs and enhances downstream analytics.

This article will guide you through the process of filling out bills.

To create a bill, navigate to the Processes tab from the main navigation menu of the app and select Bills & Expenses. Then click on the '+' button under the kanban section where you want to create your bill.

## Bill Info

If the bill is related to landed costs for your inventory, such as shipping, brokerage, or customs fees, you will need to provide the document type and the document reference number.

Otherwise, leave these fields empty.

In the initial task window, called Bill Info, you will need to provide information about the bill itself, such as which vendor has invoiced you, when (date), reference number, etc. It is recommended that you attach the file to the project using the Expense Confirmation field after filling out the necessary information.

## Service Items

Next, you will need to include the services you are paying for as part of the invoice. First, choose a service in the services field and select a quantity. The system will calculate the full amount based on the rate you previously have chosen when creating a service in the Services Catalog.

You can add multiple services as part of the same bill by clicking Add element button. This is useful if you have multiple instances of the same service that you want to capture with a single Bill.
