## Introduction

Catalogs in NeonPanel serve as fundamental repositories of data, each tailored to specific aspects of your Amazon business. While they are not tools for analytics or reporting, their accurate and detailed maintenance is crucial for the smooth operation of your business and for feeding into the systems that do provide insights and reports.

## Setting Up Your Catalogs

Effective catalog management in NeonPanel is about ensuring that each piece of data, from a product's weight to a vendor's contact information, is accurately recorded. This foundational data is vital for other NeonPanel functionalities to operate effectively.

Focus on Product and Inventory Catalogs: These catalogs are a good starting point as they directly impact several aspects of your business operations.

Accuracy is Key: Regular updates and accuracy in these catalogs ensure that your business adapts to changes efficiently.
