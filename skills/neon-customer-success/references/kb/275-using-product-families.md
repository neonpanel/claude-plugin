# Using Product Families

Category: Tips & Tricks · Article 275 · Updated 2026-02-25

In NeonPanel, you can use custom product family tags to conveniently group products for effective analysis

## Introduction

In this article, we will explore the significance of product families and how to optimize their usage.

## Why we created product families

The use of Product Family classifier is primarily for analytical data aggregation, especially in cases where managing a wide range of products across various marketplaces proves challenging. Although Amazon has classifiers like ASIN, Parent ASIN, SKU, and fnSKU, these sometimes can be inadequate since identical products can be sold under distinct SKUs and on different marketplaces.

Therefore, NeonPanel allows you to create your own Product Family list and assign each Product to a particular Product Family.

One product can only belong to one Product Family.

## How to use product families

Product families are useful as a mechanism for grouping different but related products for analytical purposes.

For instance, if you sell various bedroom accessories such as pillows for sleeping, decorative pillows, decorative blankets, and blankets for sleeping, you may create one of two sets of product family tags based on how you want to analyze your data and how you perceive your business:

1. Sleeping products and decorative products2. Blankets and pillows

Subsequently, when analyzing metrics like profitability or inventory levels for these products, you can select the relevant product family containing all the products assigned to that family, rather than manually selecting all relevant ASINs or SKUs.

This will save you time, and make it easier to generate insights about your business.
